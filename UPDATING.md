# The Shevin Travels: updating the site

The master record is `data/trips.json`. The website (`docs/`) is built from it.
The Excel workbook (`source/shevin_travel_tracker_v5.xlsx`) was the seed and is no longer updated.
Every number on the site (trips, continents, countries, states, per-person counts, the timeline)
is computed by `tools/build.py`. Nothing is typed into the page by hand.

## Adding a trip

Tell Claude, for each trip:

1. Year (a guess like `~2008` is fine) and timing (`Summer`, `Spring Break`, `Feb`)
2. Where (each city or area; Claude adds map coordinates)
3. Which of Daryl, Ivory, Jaxon, Sloane went, and who else (friends, grandma, other families)
4. Trip type: Family, Couples, International, Golf Trip, Sports Trip, Teen Tour, Personal, Guys Trip
5. Any note worth keeping

Claude then:

1. Adds one object to `trips` in `data/trips.json` (copy any existing trip as the template).
   `places` needs `name`, `lat`, `lng`, `country` (exact name from `data/geo.json`) and, for the US,
   `state` (two-letter code). A US territory uses `"country": null` and `"territory": "Puerto Rico"`.
   Give it the next unused `id` and a numeric `yearSort`.
   `travelers` is the full who-went text (used in map pop-ups and search). `others` is the short line under the
   D I J S dots in the trip log: only people beyond the four of us, like `with Brandon + Keaton`, or `""` for none.
2. Updates `meta.updated`.
3. Runs `py tools/build.py`. This runs `tools/validate.py` first and refuses to build on any error
   (unknown country or state, missing coordinates, nobody marked as traveling, and so on).
4. Opens `docs/index.html` to check the new pin, counts and trip log row.
5. Commits and pushes `main` to GitHub. GitHub Pages republishes the site within a minute or two.

## Other edits

| To change | Edit |
|---|---|
| A recurring destination | `recurring` in `data/trips.json` |
| The bucket list | `bucket`: one item per place, `who` lists whose tile it shows on; `check` lists countries or states, and the build warns once that person has been; `"us": true` puts it under USA. Keep each person to 5 international + 3 USA |
| A state or country someone visited with no trip on record | `otherVisits` |
| Footnote rules shown on the site | `meta.rules` |

After any change to `docs/styles.css` or `docs/app.js`, also run `py tools/build.py`: it stamps the file links in
`docs/index.html` with a version so visitors' browsers never mix new and old files.

## House rules (from the brief)

- Wording: `+` joins places and names in trip names and the Who line; notes use "and", never `&`. No period at the
  end of a note. The trip name is places only: one place is `City, ST` or `City, Country`; several places in one state or
  country are `Country (A, B, C)`; separate countries are joined with `+`; an event starts the note: `Fiesta Bowl: UF vs Nebraska ...`. Name lists are alphabetical
  (`with Adams, Medinas + Nardinis`); families are plural with no apostrophe. Golf and guys trips say `with buddies`. The note `Couples trip` is only for trips with other couples; a plain Daryl + Ivory trip gets a blank note.

- Florida intrastate trips are not logged. States only driven through do not count.
- Denver, Keystone, New York, Chicago, Boston, Las Vegas, New Orleans, Maine and Asheville are recurring: listed once
  in `recurring`. Repeat visits there are not logged as trips unless they were a special occasion (like the 2007
  Keystone wedding). Trips to anywhere else are logged every time, including repeats (Napa, Pebble, Pinehurst).
- US territories and non-UN places do not count as countries. Countries are out of 195
  (193 UN members plus Vatican City and Palestine). States are out of 51 (50 plus DC).
- No birth dates or home addresses anywhere on the site.
